# Chem Game v2

A chemistry lab where simulated materials follow mechanical, physical and chemical laws, with
conservation laws enforced as invariants. Design: `GAME_SPEC.md`.

## Stack

- **Game**: engine-free native C++20 (`native/core`, `native/game`) behind a C API (`native/capi/include/chem_api.h`)
- **Engine host**: Godot 4.7 (`engines/godot`; the host is one GDExtension node, `native/hosts/godot`)
- **Testing**: doctest/ctest (`chem_tests`, `lab_tests`, a ctest entry a source file); GUT 9.6.0 for the Godot host only
- **IDE**: VSCode

## Architecture

Layers with a strict dependency direction: core -> lab -> C API -> hosts. Everything but `native/hosts/godot` and
`engines/godot` is engine-free (ctest `*_no_engine`); the Godot host includes only `chem_api.h` (ctest
`godot_adapter_includes`) and the Godot project holds at most 300 lines of GDScript outside tests (ctest `gdscript_budget`).

- `native/core/include/chem/` — The simulation kernel, namespace `chem` (GAME_SPEC §3–§12). `quantity.hpp`: integer amounts (1e-12 mol) and energy (1e-9 J). `species.hpp`: species tables and `Reaction`, which cannot be constructed unbalanced. `world.hpp`: cells with private state, edges, the two primitives `transfer` and `transform`, refusals, the ledger and `StepTrace`; a rigid cell with no gas path to an elastic one is sealed (`CellMeta::sealed`, kept by the world); a drain's rate, the share of the solids its liquid carries and the solids' own rate (`set_drain`); work along an edge (`set_power`: its source a reservoir so hot its energy carries no entropy). `thermo.hpp`: what state implies (heat capacity, temperature, entropy, volume, activity from ions with Pitzer's excess, molality, affinity; a sealed cell's pressure, its energy `U + P_atm V`; Kw and the pH, Stage 43, read within the activity model's range, Stage 44). The tables grow only at their end: a new element, species or reaction is appended (a save made with fewer species still loads). `math.hpp`: the core's own `exp` and `log`. Stage 46 (GAME_SPEC §12.4), `optics.hpp`'s long-wave range: water's and glass's n + ik over 8–14 µm at 20 nm, grey emissivities, a flame's absorption, Planck's law, the band's signal in closed form, a camera's temperature from it (`brightness_kelvin`), the room's long-wave light (`lwir_energy_table`). `optics.hpp`: spectra at 1 nm (the CIE observer, D65, each species' medium, pure water as measured), narrow lines beside them, the reference colour, each light's energy table and the estimate by energy bands with moving points (GAME_SPEC §12.2); the first measurement's fixed bands (`game_bands()`, for `chem_tool study spectrum`). `sim.hpp`: `Simulation`, the fixed-step loop and its processes, applied in sequence (reactions with first-order and surface kinetics, crystal nucleation, heat conduction, work, liquid and solids down the drains, then settling: every cell to its fast equilibria such as boiling and condensation, every rigid cell exactly full; each fast equilibrium probed again only once its cell has changed, and a solid that comes out of a solution by one comes as its grains, `grain_um`: a precipitate, Stage 44); the last step's flows by process (`flows()`, GAME_SPEC §13.6). `invariants.hpp`: the invariant kernel (`check_C1`…`check_C8`, `check_T1`…`check_T3`, `check_S1`, `check_V1`…`check_V3`, `check_P1`, `check_X1`, `check_X2`, `check_all`, `Totals`). No engine, no C-library transcendentals (ctest `core_no_libm`).
- `native/datagen/` — `chem_datagen`: embeds `data/elements.json`, `species.json` (with each dissolved species' ions), `reactions.json`, `solutions.json` (Pitzer's parameters) and `data/optics/` in the core at build time, deriving atoms from formulas (`formula.hpp`) and refusing an unbalanced reaction (ctest `datagen_refuses_*`). Generated: `chem/builtin_ids.hpp` (species and reactions by name).
- `native/game/include/lab/` — The engine-free lab layer, namespace `lab` (GAME_SPEC §13). `lab.hpp`: `Lab`, a bench of equipment over one closed world, on a grid of squares (each item on a footprint), writing notices in levels of what happened (GAME_SPEC §13.4), with goals on the outbox's six slots (GAME_SPEC §13.5), and an item from the inside for the debug panel (`Lab::debug`: composition, flows, each reaction's rate, affinity and what limits it; GAME_SPEC §13.6), a `viewer` (Stage 42: the spectral viewer, no matter, its probe's link saved and not digested), driven by JSON commands (`place`, `move`, `stow`, `trash`, `restore`, `empty_trash`, `heat`, `unheat`, `valve`, `stir`, `pour`, `tip`, `attach`, `detach`, `under`, `undrain`, `submit` (to a slot of the outbox), `goal`, `ungoal`, `wait`, `wait_until`, `probe` (Stage 42); `hold`, `invert`, `unpour`, `cap` (pouring as a control: a held pour's `pour {from, rate}`, a placed pour's cap); signals: `part`, `scrap`, `sense`, `unsense`, `set`, `wire`, `unwire`, `rig`, `unrig`, `reset`, `ack`, `circuit`, `uncircuit`), with reports, events, a SHA-256 digest of the whole state (`sha256.hpp`), and save/load. `ui.hpp`: `Ui`, the screen (GAME_SPEC §13.1): a pointer in, lab commands (a frame's steps under a budget of the CPU's time, `set_budget`: the same cost at any speed, Stage 36), a draw list in two parts and the optical pass's scene out; every gesture is decided here, not in a host: the selection, the drawer of signal parts, wiring from ports, the part editor, the viewer's probe; and the view of the room (Stage 42: 1x to 16x, the room laid out anew at it; while it moves the scene stands, `view_moving`). `bubbles.hpp` (GAME_SPEC §11, Stage 45): boiling's bubbles, pure: a heated floor's convection and its onset, Rohsenow's boiling, a bubble's size, rise and condensing up the column, derived each step from the burner's flow (`data/boiling.json`), not state; `check_B1`. `contents.hpp` (GAME_SPEC §11, Stages 30 to 32): what lies in a vessel at any turn, pure: the volume under a level, the liquid's surface, the sediment's flat section and pile, a vortex's cone and the energy it stands for (`swirl`), the lip and what runs out of a round mouth (`over_brim`); one model for the lab's pour by hand, its stirring and the picture; `check_H1`, `check_H2`. `tutorial.hpp`: `Tutorial` (GAME_SPEC §13.2): steps with checks read from the lab's view and the screen (the selection, the part editor), failures, a goal, results (a species', or any quantity's start, least and most); drawn by the screen as a card and a pointer at the next gesture; strict (the card's switch, as opened: Stage 41) the screen takes only its step's gestures and holds the clock for them, free it only points. The experiments (§13.7, Stage 41): `data/experiments.json`, each a bench and its tutorial, listed by the top bar's "experiments" (`Ui::experiments`, `Ui::choose`); the roadmap of those to come, `docs/EXPERIMENTS_ROADMAP.md`. `conditions.hpp`: a quantity of an item measured from the lab's view (purity included), comparisons, the guided lists a sensing part's editor offers (kinds, materials, measures: what the item has now), and the conditions tutorials are written in (read from JSON by `src/conditions_json.cpp`). `signals.hpp` (GAME_SPEC §13.3): timers, monitors, controllers, alarms and notice parts, circuits that hold them, wires, rigs (a controller's follow its level, a valve to it, stirring by PWM) and what a monitor or a controller senses; `evaluate`, one step's signals, synchronous; `check_G1` to `check_G4`. `control.hpp`: a controller's tuning, pure: a step's response fitted (first order with a delay, or an integrator), SIMC's PI gains (Skogestad 2003) for a speed, the loop's margins; the lab tries what a controller controls on two copies of itself to get the response, two of the copies' seconds at each of its own steps (Stage 39: the trial in the save and the digest). `colour.hpp`: CIE L\*a\*b\*, CIEDE2000, the eye metric fitted to the user's marks (`eye_difference`, visible above 8), OKLCh gamut mapping, what the screen shows, sRGB, and the fixed bands' measured error. `optics_trace.hpp` (GAME_SPEC §12.3): the truth's tracer (bodies as surface patches, 3D rays, float64, on the CPU; written apart from the pass's own, which is in the per-pixel source). `optics_scene.hpp`: the renderer's scene from live state (`optics_compose`: bodies, spectra at 1 nm, lines, the room light's energy table, each body's shape as surfaces with how it lies and what lies in it, the wall as a table of exact integrals; a screen, a slab that emits its image through three primaries (Stage 42: the images' buffer, `check_O17`; `optics_point`, one ray's light at a point, and `check_O18`, the view a similarity); `OpticsBody::layer`: what is held stands a gap nearer the eye, in one world of the screen's pixels; Stage 46: every row a long-wave segment after the visible rows, two lights, each body's temperature (Kirchhoff in every shading, glass absorbing in both tracers), the grid on the room's own squares at any view (no body left out), bodies hidden from a sensor's rays, a sensor's display (`OpticsBody::Sensor`, `OpticsRelay`: the long-wave light at its frame, a record a pixel in a pool, `check_O21` to `check_O25`, `optics_relay_truth`), the debug infrared view (`Ui::set_infrared`); the rectangles of the bodies that bend, where the passes that trace run), the reference renderer (the truth at a pixel, and `OpticsFrames`: the renderer's own frames, each pixel's history), `optics_compare` (O4), the truth of a pixel's area by its own tracer (`optics_area_truth`), `check_O1`, `check_O2`, `check_O7`, `check_O8`; the one reading (Stage 29, the only one from Stage 35: `docs/TDD_35_ONE.md`), a pixel's own rays traced in the pass, a pixel its beam's reading and the mean of its rays' corrections: `check_O9`, `check_O10`, `check_O11`; and (Stage 33, the pass made fast: `docs/TDD_33_FAST.md`) a pixel's beams kept while its shape key is the same and the corners of neighbouring pixels shared (`OpticsFrames::keep`, `share`), `check_O12`, `check_O13`; and the same source in single precision on the CPU (`src/optics_single.cpp`: every sum a float's, the GPU's tolerances; `src/optics_pixel_host.inc` is what each arithmetic wraps the source in, `src/optics_frames.hpp` the frames behind `OpticsFrames`, which takes `single`): `check_O15` (a ray), `check_O16` (a pixel's settled colour; Stage 35). `play.hpp`: a play, gestures as data a frame at a time (`data/plays/`), what `make pass-cost` times and the tests count (Stage 42: a step may set the bench out, turn the wheel, and ask the host for a picture). `spectrometer.hpp` (Stage 42): the spectral viewer's probe, pure: a disc's light at every nanometre, rays through points uniform over it, coarse first, settling to the disc's integral (`check_O19`); its screen's picture (an RGB8 image: a column a nanometre, the rainbow of each wavelength as the game shows a spike of it). `view.hpp` (Stage 42): the view of the room, pure: a scale and the layout's point at the room's corner, zoomed about a point, moved, clamped. `native/game/shader/optics_pixel.glsl`: the per-pixel method (GAME_SPEC §12.2), one source compiled as the Godot host's compute shaders and, on the CPU, as the reference in float64 (`src/optics_reference.cpp`) and whole in single precision (`src/optics_single.cpp`); it keeps to what GLSL and C++ read alike. A pixel is the light of all its rays (§12.3): it traces its beam (its corners and its middle at four wavelengths) through the bodies' surfaces, reads the wall's table exactly over the beam's footprint, and adds the mean of a few traced rays' corrections. On the GPU a pass costs what its shader could do, done or not, so the host compiles the source four times: the pass over the whole screen without the tracing (`OPTICS_SCREEN_PASS`), over the rectangles of the bodies that bend a pass for the corners pixels share (`OPTICS_CORNER_PASS`) and one for the pixels' beams (`OPTICS_BEAM_PASS`), and (Stage 42) a view's change (`OPTICS_RESAMPLE_PASS`: while the view moves the pixels carried from the settled ones, at rest each pixel's state carried once; the reference's `OpticsFrames::resample`, `O20`).
- `native/capi/` — `chem_api.h`: the game behind a plain C ABI (size-prefixed structs, borrowed views, statuses).
- `native/hosts/headless/` — `chem_tool`: the game without an engine: `version`, `tables` (the built-in species and reactions), `run SESSION [--record|--check FILE]` (a session script through the C API, every invariant checked, the measured results and the lab's warnings printed), `bench SESSION` (the same unchecked, timed), `study <name>` (a stage's physics alone against its closed form, on the core directly: `cooling`, `decomposition`, `balloon`, `distillation`, `dissolution`, `crystals`, `solution_heat`, `neutralisation`, `precipitation`; `spectrum`, the band-count measurement; `tilt` and `vortex`, what lies in a vessel against its closed forms; `speed`, the screen's frame on the CPU at 1x, 60x and 600x and under a budget, scene by scene).
- `native/hosts/godot/` — The Godot host: `ChemLab`, a `Node2D` that reads the bench, forwards the mouse, calls a frame and paints: the draw list's first part, the optical pass (a compute shader over the screen and two over the rectangles `cg_optics` names, the corners' and the beams', `optics_compute.cpp`: the per-pixel source with its state in four float images and each pixel's kept beam on the GPU, fed `cg_ui_optics`, shown by a child `TextureRect`), the list's second part (`cg_ui_*`); the wheel, a pinch, two fingers and the middle button for the room's view, and pictures a play asks for (`LAB_SHOTS`; Stage 42). No game logic.
- `engines/godot/` — The Godot project. `make native` builds the extension into `engines/godot/bin` and copies `data/` into `engines/godot/data` and the pixel shader source into `engines/godot/shaders`.
- `data/` — Source-of-truth data: `elements.json`, `species.json`, `reactions.json` (embedded at build), `benches/` (equipment and what it holds), `sessions/` (scripts), `tutorials/` (the demo and the experiments as steps with checks), `experiments.json` (the catalogue the screen lists). `bench/sessions/` — the sessions' golden records (a digest after every command).
- `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` — every physical value with its source and confidence. A value is used only once its row is verified.

## Testing

The protocol: `docs/WORKFLOW.md`.

```bash
make check                      # the engine-free build, then every ctest entry
make check AREA=core            # core | lab | all
make check TC="sweeps*"         # one doctest filter in chem_tests (CHEM_SWEEP_WORLDS=n scales the conservation sweep)
make quick                      # between commits: only what a change can affect (result cache)
make gate                       # before every commit: core, ctest, the extension, the Godot suite, goldens unchanged
SKIP_GODOT=1 make gate          # every engine-free gate
make gates                      # before a merge or a stage close: uncached, plus ASan+UBSan
make native                     # the Godot extension, data copy, import
./run_tests_parallel.sh         # the Godot host's GUT suite
make run                        # the game (the Godot host)
make screenshots                # pictures of the host through the demo -> build/screenshots (one with a vessel held over another), the GPU's settled optical pass against the truth on the eye metric (O4; `LAB_O4_PRINT=1`: each pixel off, with what its frames come to on the CPU in double and in single precision) and its cost (headless first, then a window for a few seconds, time-limited)
make pass-cost                  # the optical pass's cost on the GPU (Stage 33): a play (data/plays/cost.json: settled, a vessel carried, poured by hand, stirred) timed mark by mark, then every history starting again; headless first, then a window, time-limited
LAB_SHOTS=$PWD/build/screenshots make pass-cost PLAY=data/plays/viewer.json   # the spectral viewer and the room's view on the GPU, timed, with pictures (Stage 42)
make demo                       # the 3-stage demo, headless: the commands and what was measured
make demo-bench                 # its step cost
make sessions-check             # the sessions against their golden records (make sessions re-records: a behaviour change)
```

- Native tests: doctest `TEST_CASE`s in `native/tests/*.cpp` (`chem_tests`) and `native/tests/game/*.cpp` (`lab_tests`); names end with their stage, `(Stage N)`. Each `chem_tests` source file is a ctest entry and each `lab_tests` test case one (`lab_tests.<file>.<n>`; a `// ctest: parts N` or `// ctest: threads N` line before a case splits its loop or gives it threads), run side by side; a test that holds many pixels to the brute-force truth leans on the reference's own threads (`LAB_THREADS`) and, under the sanitizers, samples sparsely (`sparse()`, `tests/support/sparse.hpp`): `docs/WORKFLOW.md`.
- Godot host tests: `engines/godot/tests/test_*.gd`, extending `GutTest`. The screen itself is tested natively (`native/tests/game/test_ui.cpp`), through `Ui::locate`.
- **A script that runs on Godot's own thread never waits unbounded**: every loop in `engines/godot/tools/` has a limit, and a windowed Godot started from a shell is given a time limit (`perl -e 'alarm 90; exec @ARGV' godot …`). An unbounded wait froze the window once.
- **Invariants over examples**: a property goes in the catalogue (GAME_SPEC §18) as a named check with a self-test that proves it fires on a corrupted trace.
- **Fetched data is documented and held to its source**: any data fetched from outside and used to change or fix the code (a measured or fitted value, a constant, a calibration) is documented in full (`docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`: the source, what was read, the fit, the confidence) and has a gate test that runs the model at the source's own conditions and checks what the source measured (the measured kinetics: `native/tests/test_measured.cpp`), so a change to the data or to the code that reads it fails the gate. Every number in `data/` has an entry in `data/sources.json` (measured with its tests and doc rows, calibrated with why, or design); ctest `data_sources` fails on one without, so new data cannot enter undocumented or untested.
- **Regression policy**: after Stage N, all tests from Stages 0 through N pass before proceeding.

## Stage Workflow

1. Spec first: the stage's section of `GAME_SPEC.md`, its invariants, its rows of `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` verified
2. Tests, then the kernel green under `make check`; `make gate` before each commit (small green commits on a branch)
3. The lab layer, the C API, the hosts, as the stage needs
4. Goldens: a changed golden or session record is a behaviour change; bring the evidence and get the user's approval before re-recording
5. Docs: the stage's TDD doc (`docs/TDD_NN_<TOPIC>.md`, phase table), CHANGELOG, GAME_SPEC's version; `make gates` closes the stage
6. The user tests interactively (or reviews the headless report) and gives pass/fail feedback
7. Failing interactive tests become automated tests; fix and re-run until all pass

**No stage is complete without the user's sign-off.**

Commits: `Stage N: <behaviour sentence>` on a branch; merged to `main` as `Merge Stage N: <summary> (GAME_SPEC x.y.z)`.

## Code Style

- C++20, tabs, short comments naming the GAME_SPEC section
- Conserved quantities are integers (amounts in 1e-12 mol, energy in 1e-9 J); derived quantities are float64 in SI units
- Cell state changes only through `transfer` and `transform` (GAME_SPEC §4.3)
- Error handling: debug builds assert and abort on an invariant violation; release builds report and continue (`chem/error.hpp`; the C API returns statuses)
